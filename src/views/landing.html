Problem statement

So… we just email .html files?

Your prototype is done. Now what — email a zip file? Record a Loom? The moment you want to put a working click-through in front of a teammate, a stakeholder, or a customer, the workflow falls apart: zipped folders, Teams threads with screenshots, feedback scattered across five tools.

There has to be a better way. ProtoLab is it.

  • Pin-point feedback

  • Controlled access
    Invite by email. Track who opened the prototype. Revoke when an experiment ends.
  • Conversations in context
